\chapter{Low-N Subgroup Diagnostic Notes}
\label{app:low-n-subgroups}

\textbf{Diagnostic only, not a fairness audit.} This appendix
extends Chapter~\ref{chap:subgroup-diagnostics} with the DECTE subgroup rows that
did not meet the main-table sample-size threshold and were therefore
excluded from the headline subgroup tables. The same wording contract
as Chapter~\ref{chap:subgroup-diagnostics} applies to every row listed here:
these are descriptive point-estimates on very small sub-slices, they
carry no per-subgroup confidence interval, and they are not evidence
for or against any fairness claim.

Chapter~\ref{chap:subgroup-diagnostics}, Section~\ref{sec:subgroup-protocol} sets the main-table
threshold at \textbf{at least 10 bonafide and at least 10 spoof files}
per subgroup on the joined DECTE XTTS held-out slice. Subgroups
below that threshold are computed and written to the CSV output
(\texttt{results/}\allowbreak\texttt{subgroup\_diagnostics/}\allowbreak\texttt{decte\_subgroup\_metrics.csv})
but flagged as \texttt{below\_n\_threshold = True} so that they cannot
enter the chapter's headline claims. Table~\ref{tab:appendix-low-n-subgroups}
records the low-N rows for the two age bands that surfaced in the
joined slice.

These rows are reported for transparency only: they let a reader
verify that Chapter~\ref{chap:subgroup-diagnostics}'s main-table exclusions were the
result of a fixed pre-registered threshold rather than a
post-hoc filter. No per-subgroup confidence intervals were computed
for any of these rows, and the small per-class counts make even
point-estimate delta EER values statistically uninformative on their
own. Consistent with the wording contract in Chapter~\ref{chap:subgroup-diagnostics},
Section~\ref{sec:subgroup-interpretation}, no statement in the thesis attributes
detector behaviour to any of the excluded subgroups, and neither
this appendix nor the CSV output supports fairness-style claims of
the form ``the detector is biased against group X''.

The full CSV (including the per-speaker breakdown noted in
Chapter~\ref{chap:subgroup-diagnostics}, Section~\ref{sec:subgroup-low-n}) is the source of record for
these numbers.
