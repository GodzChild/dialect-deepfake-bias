\chapter{VCTK Control Slate}
\label{app:vctk-slate}

VCTK 0.92 is used throughout this thesis as the standard-accent
English control corpus against which DECTE dialectal / interview-%
style speech is compared (Chapter~\ref{chap:data-methodology}, Section~\ref{sec:methodology-corpora-vctk}).
The selection was deterministic: after the layout-verification and
loose-English-accent filter described in Section~\ref{sec:methodology-corpora-vctk},
33 VCTK speakers were eligible, of which the alphabetically first
20 with at least 8 usable utterances each formed the slate. This
appendix records those 20 speaker identifiers and the aggregate
composition already summarised in the main text.

The full per-speaker VCTK metadata (per-speaker gender, age band,
region within the loose-English filter, and utterance identifiers)
lives in the VCTK 0.92 distribution's \texttt{speaker-info.txt}, which
is not redistributed with this repository. Per the assembly plan's
scope, the appendix records the selected speaker identifiers only
and defers per-speaker metadata to the VCTK distribution itself.

\paragraph{Aggregate composition (already documented in the main text).}
The 20-speaker slate has the following aggregate properties, as
reported in the findings log:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Total speakers:} 20.
    \item \textbf{Region:} 13 southern (Southern England / London /
        Surrey / SE England / SW England) and 7 non-southern
        (Manchester, Cumbria, Stockton-on-Tees, Birmingham,
        Nottingham, Staffordshire). No speaker is from Newcastle;
        there is no direct region overlap with DECTE.
    \item \textbf{Gender:} 11 female / 9 male at the speaker level.
    \item \textbf{Age band:} 19 of 20 in the 21--30 band; one in
        31--40. This concentration is one of the axes on which
        DECTE and VCTK differ simultaneously and is one of the
        reasons the observed DECTE-vs-VCTK gap is reported as a
        \emph{dialect / domain gap} rather than a pure dialect
        gap (Chapter~\ref{chap:discussion}, Section~\ref{sec:discussion-wording-contract}).
    \item \textbf{Spoof coverage:} 100 XTTS v2 and 100 OpenVoice v2
        spoofs, all generated from utterances drawn from the same
        20-speaker slate (per-cell breakdown in
        Appendix~\ref{app:spoof-generation-counts}).
\end{itemize}
