\chapter{Mitigation Parameter Details}
\label{app:mitigation-parameters}

Mitigation v2 (Chapter~\ref{chap:mitigation}) is a \emph{controlled adaptation}
of the AuralGuard-AASISTPP detector, not a re-training. Only a small
part of the model is allowed to move during fine-tuning; the rest is
frozen at its baseline-checkpoint values. This appendix records the
exact set of modules that are trainable in v2 and the corresponding
parameter counts.

Concretely, at the start of the v2 training run the fine-tuning
script reports \textbf{31,372 trainable parameters} and
\textbf{268,104 frozen parameters}, i.e.\ approximately \textbf{10.5\,\%}
of the model's total parameters are adapted; the remaining
$\sim$89.5\,\% retain their baseline-checkpoint values exactly.

Training hyperparameters used with the modules above are documented
in Chapter~\ref{chap:data-methodology}, Section~\ref{sec:methodology-mitigation-config}:
5 epochs, batch size 4, learning rate $3 \times 10^{-5}$,
\texttt{num\_workers = 0} (Windows), single GPU, AdamW over
\texttt{filter(lambda p: p.requires\_grad, model.parameters())} so
gradients only flow to the modules listed as \textit{Yes} above.

The choice of these specific modules --- the final heterogeneous-GAT
stage plus the three AuralGuard task heads --- is discussed in
Chapter~\ref{chap:data-methodology}, Section~\ref{sec:methodology-mitigation-config}:
the fine-tune is allowed to shift the top-of-backbone view (the
graph-attention interactions between spectral and temporal pooled
representations) plus the classifier heads, while every low- and
mid-level acoustic representation is left intact. The resulting
adaptation is a \emph{controlled adaptation}, not a production
deployment: the mitigated checkpoint's residual DECTE XTTS EER of
23.26\,\% is well above any operational usability threshold
(Chapter~\ref{chap:mitigation}, Section~\ref{sec:mitigation-limitations}).
