## 7. Formatting and template plan

**Final output template**: **to decide**.

Two viable candidates:

- **LaTeX + a JKU / ICP thesis class** (`\documentclass{...}`
  provided by the institute, or a general `book`-based
  scientific-thesis class such as `KOMA-Script scrbook`). LaTeX
  is preferred for anything with mathematical notation or
  large-table typesetting, and it produces the most polished
  PDF. Requires the JKU / ICP institute template package if one
  is mandated.
- **Word (Microsoft Word / LibreOffice)** with an ICP style
  template. Easier for supervisor collaboration if the
  supervisor prefers Word tracked changes; less polished for
  code listings and complex tables.

**Recommendation**: LaTeX if the institute supplies (or
tolerates) a `.cls`; otherwise Word.

**Template requirements to confirm** with the supervisor before
the polish pass ends:

- Title-page layout (institute vs university logo; supervisor
  vs advisor field; submission-date format).
- Bibliography style (numbered `[1]` vs author-year `(Doe,
  2020)` vs one of the ICP-approved styles).
- Table / figure caption position (above vs below).
- Sectioning depth (`Chapter → Section → Subsection` vs deeper).
- Whether the code-listing font in `\texttt{...}` blocks
  (or Word `Consolas`) should be a specific mono font.
- Page-number convention (Arabic from Chapter 1, Roman for
  front matter).
- Length ceiling: JKU BSc theses typically ~ 40–60 pages of
  body; current word count of ~ 29k is within that once
  formatted.

## 9. Risk checklist

Grouped by risk category. Each item is a *check* that must pass
before the thesis is considered submission-ready.

### 9.1 Overclaiming checks

- No occurrence of `"pure dialect bias"` anywhere in Chapters
  1–9 (grep clean already; must remain so after edits).
- No occurrence of `"fairness solved"` or `"fairness fix"`
  anywhere in Chapters 1–9.
- No occurrence of `"production-ready detector"` or `"deployment-
  ready detector"` claiming that the mitigated checkpoint is
  one. Only allowed in the *"is not one"* framing of Chapter 8,
  Section 8.9 and Chapter 9, Section 9.7.
- No occurrence of `"first ever"`, `"first-of-a-kind"`, or
  `"novel contribution"` outside the explicit non-claim in
  Chapter 1, Section 1.6.
- No causal or fairness wording (`"biased against"`,
  `"unfair to"`, `"gender/age/era caused"`) outside Chapter 7,
  Section 7.10 and Chapter 8, Section 8.9's explicit
  "do NOT write" callouts.

### 9.2 Reproducibility checks

- **External resources.** Chapter 3, Section 3.9.6 and
  Appendix A list four external artefacts required for
  end-to-end reproduction (DECTE access, VCTK download,
  AuralGuard baseline checkpoint, spoof generator checkpoints).
  These must remain in the final thesis explicitly, not silently
  dropped during polish.
- **Absolute local paths.** The drafts occasionally include
  absolute Windows paths like
  `C:\Users\AYO\Desktop\JKU\Extra Semester\THESIS AND PRACTICAL\...`
  for the AuralGuard project location and the baseline
  checkpoint. These should be **anonymised or made relative**
  before submission (e.g., `<AURALGUARD_ROOT>/results/.../best.pt`).
  Applies to Chapters 3 and 4 specifically.
- **User-name in git commits and elsewhere.** Verify no
  personally-identifying information beyond the author name on
  the title page leaks into the printed thesis.

### 9.3 Consistency checks

- **DECTE / VCTK wording.** Always "DECTE" (all caps), not
  "Decte" or "decte". Always "VCTK" or "VCTK 0.92". Always
  "DECTE and VCTK" (in that order) when both are named.
- **XTTS / OpenVoice wording.** Always "XTTS v2" (with the
  version, not just "XTTS"). Always "OpenVoice v2". Always
  "XTTS v2 and OpenVoice v2" (in that order) when both are
  named.
- **AASIST / AuralGuard naming.** The *architecture* is AASIST.
  The *wrapper* is AuralGuard-AASISTPP. The *checkpoint* is
  the AuralGuard-AASISTPP baseline. The *primary detector* in
  this thesis is the AuralGuard-AASISTPP wrapper around AASIST.
  Chapters 3 and 4 must be internally consistent; Chapters 5–9
  can refer to it as "the primary detector" or "AASIST" without
  the full wrapper name once introduced.
- **EER / AUC formatting.** Always "EER" not "eer"; always "AUC"
  not "auc". Percentages formatted with a space before `%`
  (e.g. `40.70 %`, not `40.70%`), matching the Chapter 5 draft
  convention. Bootstrap CIs formatted with a comma inside `[...]`
  (e.g. `[−28.49, −9.30] pp`), matching the Chapter 6 draft
  convention. Signs use the mathematical minus (`−`) not the
  hyphen (`-`), matching the Chapter 5 draft convention.
- **Contract phrases.** The four contract phrases used
  throughout — *dialect / domain gap*, *generator-specific
  corpus interaction*, *cross-detector replication*,
  *controlled adaptation* — must appear at least once each in
  Chapters 5, 6, 7, 8, 9. (Chapter 1 uses all four; Chapter 2
  uses "cross-domain generalisation" as an ancillary phrase.)
